Is Bull Riding More About the Rider or the Bull?

In professional bull riding, the bull and the rider are scored together out of 100 points. There are two judges standing on either side of the arena to see both sides of the ride. Each judge scores both the rider and the bull out of 25 points.

To produce a higher scoring ride, is the rider typically scored higher, or is the bull scored higher?

This scatterplot compares rider scores and bull scores for each scored ride. It helps show whether higher rider scores tend to occur alongside higher bull scores. 

The correlation of the two scores does indicate a positive relationship between the scores, meaning that higher rider scores tend to be alongside higher bull scores.

Based on the average scores, it is shown that riders often contribute higher scores than the bulls in qualified rides. However, both athletes play a big role in determining the final score. 


Which Riders tend to put up the highest scores?


Rather than looking into one exceptionally higher score, I compared the consistency of the riders throughout the Velocity Tour in 2025. To make the data more meaningful I only included riders that had five or more qualified rides.

Code 
top_riders <- bullriding |>
  group_by(Rider) |>
  summarise(
    Average_Score = mean(Rider_Score, na.rm = TRUE),
    Attempts = n()
  ) |>
  filter(Attempts >= 5) |>
  arrange(desc(Average_Score)) |>
  slice_head(n = 10)

ggplot(top_riders,
       aes(x = reorder(Rider, Average_Score),
           y = Average_Score)) +
  geom_col() +
  coord_flip() +
  labs(
    title = "The Top 10 Highest-Scoring Riders",
    subtitle = "Minimum of five scored rides",
    x = "Rider",
    y = "Average Rider Score"
  ) +
  theme_minimal()

These results show which riders produced the highest average scores among those with at least five qualified rides. 




The 2025 Pendleton Whisky Velocity Tour results show that bull riding is a sport that depends on the performances of two athletes. Rider scores and bull scores have a positive relationship and both contribute to the final score. 

Although the riders in this dataset did contribute higher scores than the bulls, that does not mean one athlete is more important than the other. A high-scoring ride only comes with a combination of rider ability and bull performance.


Overall, the riders reinforce what makes bull riding a unique sport. It takes two athletes to produce a great ride.
